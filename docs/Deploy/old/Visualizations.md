## Key Insights

1. **Profitability Structure**:
   - Business Basic shows highest base margin (28%)
   - Personal Pro needs most optimization (-35%)
   - Add-ons critical for Personal Premium and Pro plans

2. **Cost Distribution**:
   - Infrastructure dominates all plans (56-77%)
   - Storage cost proportion increases with tier
   - Management costs decrease with scale

3. **Revenue Optimization**:
   - Business Enterprise has highest add-on potential (100%)
   - Personal Basic has lowest but safest margins
   - Business Premium shows best balance

4. **Scaling Efficiency**:
   - Business Basic most efficient (2.08 revenue/cost)
   - Personal Pro least efficient (1.00 revenue/cost)
   - Enterprise plans benefit most from scale

5. **Break-even Points**:
   - Personal Basic: 15 customers (75% utilization)
   - Business Premium: 8 customers (80% utilization)
   - Enterprise: 25 customers (83% utilization)
